## Purpose

HR is evaluating two pre-employment testing platforms, Mettl and Testlify. Before we select one, we would like to know which tests your team would use when hiring. Please review the summary below and reply to the questions at the end by [CONFIRM: response deadline].

## Summary of the two platforms

| | Mettl | Testlify |
|---|---|---|
| Typical test length | 30 to 65 minutes | 10 to 25 minutes |
| Tests grouped by department | Yes: Operations, Sales, Human Resources, Marketing | Not listed |
| Tests matched to experience level | Yes, for most tests | Not listed |
| Personality and behavior tests | Personality Mapping, Personality Map (Forced Choice), Motivation Inventory, EQ Test | Big Five (SMART), DISC, Enneagram, Cultural Fit, Leadership Style, Self-Esteem, Sales Profiler |
| Custom tests | Can set difficulty, combine skills, add our own questions, request a tailor-made test | AI-assisted builder that creates tests from selected skills and experience |
| Interview tools | Not listed | Chat simulations and AI-scored video and audio interviews |
| Pricing | Not yet received | Not yet received |

## Tests available for the roles we hire

**Sales and Operations**
- Mettl: Aptitude Test for Sales Jobs (50 min), Operations Manager (60 min), Cognitive Abilities Assessment (60 min), Motivation Inventory (35 min), Spoken English, Reading Comprehension (45 min), Listening Skills (15 min).
- Testlify: Sales Profiler (20 min), Communication Ability (15 min), Problem Solving (10 min), Decision Making (10 min), Adaptability (15 min).

**HR and Marketing**
- Mettl: HR Executives (40 min), HR Manager (65 min), Marketing Executive (40 min). Mettl notes that different marketing tests are available by role.
- Testlify: no HR or marketing role tests are listed. General tests such as Communication Ability and Cultural Fit are available.

**Executive Assistants, Research Associates and Admin Assistants**
- Neither platform lists a test designed for these roles. General tests that may apply include:
  - Mettl: Verbal Reasoning (45 min), Verbal Ability (30 min), Numerical Reasoning (35 min), Critical Thinking (30 min), Logical Reasoning (30 min), Reading Comprehension (45 min).
  - Testlify: Problem Solving (10 min), Communication Ability (15 min), English Language tests, and a category of typing and software tests whose contents are not yet listed.

## Questions for you

1. Which of the tests above would you use for the roles on your team?
2. What is the longest test a candidate should take for your roles: under 15 minutes, 15 to 30 minutes, or up to 60 minutes?
3. Would personality or behavior tests be useful for your roles? If so, which?
4. Would you use AI-scored video or audio interviews? (HR will review legal and privacy requirements before any use.)
5. Is there a skill you need to test that is not listed here?

Thank you for your input. HR will share a recommendation once responses and pricing are in.

Regards,
HR Team
