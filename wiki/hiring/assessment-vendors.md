# Candidate Assessment Vendors: Mettl and Testlify

## Summary
This page compares two pre-employment testing platforms, Mettl and Testlify, from a single comparison document. The first part of the document lists a detailed test catalog with test type, experience level, difficulty, duration and question count, grouped by category and by department. The second part lists Testlify's offering in less detail. The source contains no pricing, contract terms or recommendation, so no vendor choice has been made.

### Differences visible in the source
- **Fact:** Mettl's tests are mostly 30 to 65 minutes; Testlify's listed tests are 10 to 25 minutes.
- **Fact:** only the Testlify section mentions AI-built assessments and AI-scored video or audio interviews.
- **Fact:** only the Mettl section lists tests by department and by experience level.
